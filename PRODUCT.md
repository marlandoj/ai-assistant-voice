# AI Assistant Voice

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Zo owners speak with their selected assistant persona and use workspace tools while keeping control of actions. The existing private PWA is used on desktop and phone browsers. Success means clear conversation, accurate task results and understandable approval decisions.

## Design principles

Preserve the existing portrait, theme settings, transcript, archive and persona selection. Keep Realtime as the default. GPT-Live is explicitly started and stopped, with connection and backend progress shown separately. Show complete action details before approval and offer rejection or cancellation. Use conventional labeled controls and at least 44px touch targets for new actions.

## Personality and anti-references

Calm, direct, useful. Avoid dashboards, decorative status panels, unexplained technical controls, and automatic billable Live sessions. Retain existing visual tokens and system typography.
